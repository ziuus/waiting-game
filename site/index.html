A game that
waits.

Full-screen transparent overlay. Six game modes. Global leaderboards. Zero idle cost. Play while you build, wait, or deploy.

⬇ Install Source →

One binary, one keypress

No runtime deps. No config files. Just launch and play.

~/ waiting-game
$ ./waiting-game
[game] loading overlay…
[game] mode: dino-runner
[game] score: 2,450 — press H to hide
[player] Zius ranked #1 on leaderboard
[game] ✓ score submitted
$ ▊

Six ways to play

Each mode has its own physics, scoring, and look. Click any game to see screenshots.

🦖

Dino Runner

Classic runner with jump physics. Speed increases as you dodge cacti and birds.

🐦

Flappy Bird

Momentum-based flight. Navigate neon pipe gaps.

🌀

Gravity Runner

Flip gravity to dodge spikes. Survival through reversal.

🐍

Cyber Snake

Grid-based snake. Collect neon food, grow longer.

🧱

Neon Breakout

Paddle-and-ball. Clear brick waves for points.

🛡️

Defender

Side-scrolling shooter. Destroy enemies, survive.

Built for downtime

Waiting for a build, deploy, or meeting to start? That's game time.

🪟

Transparent overlay

Renders on top of your desktop. Your workspace stays visible underneath.

⚡

Near-zero idle cost

Negligible CPU and RAM when hidden. No impact on work.

⌨️

Instant toggle

Hide with a keypress. Bind to any hotkey in your compositor.

📊

Global leaderboards

Every score submits. Track your rank across all players.

🎮

Six game modes

Dino, Flappy, Gravity, Snake, Breakout, Defender — unique physics each.

🎨

Customizable roster

Enable, disable, rename, and theme every game through config.

AppImage

Single binary, no dependencies.

$ curl -L -o waiting-game.AppImage https://github.com/ziuus/waiting-game/releases/download/v0.4.2/waiting-game_0.4.2_amd64.AppImage
$ chmod +x waiting-game.AppImage
$ ./waiting-game.AppImage

Hyprland toggle

Add to hyprland.conf:

bind = SUPER SHIFT, G, exec, pkill waiting-game || ~/Applications/waiting-game-0.4.2.AppImage
